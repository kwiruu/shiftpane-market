## The phone can't connect

- **Same Wi-Fi.** The phone and the PC have to be on the same network. Guest networks often keep devices apart.
- **The right adapter.** With a VPN, WSL or VirtualBox, the QR code may name the wrong address. Pick your Wi-Fi or Ethernet adapter from the **Network** menu under the QR code.
- **Windows Firewall.** If the firewall would block phones from reaching the PC, the Devices page says so. Allow ShiftPane on private networks, and make sure Windows counts your home network as private.
- **The certificate.** The first time, the browser warns about the PC's certificate. That's expected: accept it once.

## The code is refused

Codes change every ten minutes, and each pairs one phone. After five wrong tries the PC stops taking codes until the next one: press **New code** on the Devices page.

## No monitor appears

- **Windows:** Virtual Display Driver has to be installed, once. See [Install ShiftPane](/docs/install#the-virtual-monitor).
- **The split.** On the **Widgets** split there's no monitor at all. Pick another split on the phone or on the Phone layout page.

## A widget says "Waiting for the PC"

The widget has no data yet. PC stats come from the native helper; if it isn't running, the board has nothing to show. Restart the desktop app.
