The widget board is what the phone shows when it isn't a monitor: tables of widgets on a black screen, sized to the phone. The PC keeps the layout and sends the phone finished data, so the board looks the same on every phone you pair.

## Lay it out from the desktop

Open **Phone layout** in the desktop app. It previews the phone you picked in the sidebar.

- **Tables** hold the widgets. Drag tables to reorder them (or <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>↑</Kbd> and <Kbd>↓</Kbd>).
- **Add a widget** by clicking it in the gallery on the right, or by dragging it onto the phone.
- **Move a widget** to another table by dragging it onto that table's row.
- **Pick a widget** on the phone to change its settings in the editor above the gallery.

The default board is four tables: Calendar and CPU, Memory and an analog clock, Network, then a PC overview.

## Built-in widgets

ShiftPane ships with a clock, an analog clock, a calendar, now playing, and PC stats (CPU, memory and network on their own, or together). The PC stats come from the native helper, which reads the OS's counters; the phone only ever gets the numbers.

## More widgets

More widgets are in the [gallery](/widgets) (**Open the gallery** on the app's **Widgets** page brings you there). **Install in ShiftPane** on a widget's page opens the desktop app, which shows what the widget can do before it installs it. Installed widgets appear on the **Widgets** page and in the same picker, after the built-in ones. See [Making widgets](/docs/making-widgets) to make your own.
