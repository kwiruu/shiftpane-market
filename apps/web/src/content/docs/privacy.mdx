## The app

ShiftPane works on your network. Your phone talks to your PC directly, over your Wi-Fi.

- **What leaves your PC:** video of the virtual monitor (or the monitor you chose to show), and finished widget data, to your paired phones only.
- **What stays on your PC:** everything else. Secrets you give widgets are stored encrypted on the PC and only go to the widget that declared them, and from there only to the hosts it declared.
- **Paired phones:** the PC keeps a fingerprint of each phone's key, not the key.
- **No telemetry.** The app doesn't report how you use it.

Installing and updating widgets downloads from the gallery's file server, and the app checks the revocation list every six hours. Neither sends anything about you beyond the request itself. The app only talks to the gallery's API when you share a widget from it.

## This site

The site has no analytics and no ads, and loads nothing from other sites.

When accounts open, signing in uses GitHub. We'll keep your GitHub login, name and avatar to show you as an author, and a session cookie to keep you signed in. Widgets you upload are public once approved, along with their source.
