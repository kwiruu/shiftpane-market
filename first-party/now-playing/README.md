The track playing on your PC, from whichever app is playing it, laid out like the media widget in the ShiftPane app: a cover colored from the title, the track and artist, which app is playing it, and buttons to go back, pause and skip.

## Permissions

- **Sees what's playing.** The `media` source tells it the title, artist and app. That says something about what you're doing, so installing asks first.
- **Presses three media buttons:** previous, play or pause, and next. The phone sends the name of the action, never a command, and the app only knows these few.

It has no network access, so what you listen to stays between your PC and your phone.
